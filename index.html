About Me

I’m Adetayo Okunoye, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Georgia. I am working on AI systems of foundation models that make glycoscience faster, more reliable, and explainable— fusing large language models with knowledge graphs and structured reasoning. I am advised by Dr. I. Budak Arpinar and co-advised by Dr. John A. Miller.

My current work focuses on multimodal learning for glycan structure elucidation from MS/MS spectra and mechanism-aware biomarker discovery. I care deeply about tools that scientists can trust: methods that are transparent by design, grounded in biochemistry, and reproducible in real labs.

Research Interests

  • Multimodal Foundation Models for Glycoinformatics
  • Neuro-Symbolic AI for Explainable Biomarker Discovery
  • AI-Driven de novo Glycan Structure Elucidation
  • Knowledge Graphs for Biochemical Reasoning (RDF/SHACL/SPARQL)
  • Scaling & Optimization of AI Inference Systems
  • Responsible AI, Causality & Model Reliability
