Research Overview

Thesis Topic: Provably Constrained Probabilistic Operators for Scientific Simulation

My work aims to embed physical constraints directly into the architecture of probabilistic operator models to create data-efficient, trustworthy, and interpretable surrogate models for partial differential equations (PDEs), with guaranteed adherence to fundamental scientific laws.

Research Phases

  • Phase 1 – Foundation & Tractable Testbed: Literature review, Navier–Stokes setup, baseline FNO & PINO.
  • Phase 2 – Hard-Constrained Deterministic Core: Divergence-free Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) ensuring ∇·u = 0.
  • Phase 3 – Probabilistic Leap: Integration of constrained architectures with generative models (e.g. cVAE).
  • Phase 4 – Demonstration & Abstraction: Extending to other PDEs and building an open-source constraint layer library.
