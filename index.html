Research Overview

Glycomics lags behind genomics/proteomics because MS/MS analysis suffers from isomeric ambiguity, sparse libraries, lab variability, and manual, subjective interpretation. We propose an integrated multimodal AI GlycoLLM (encodes spectra + glycan graphs + metadata), GlycoKG (biosynthetic constraints), and GlycoGoT (structured reasoning), that jointly reasons across modalities to produce accurate, confidence-aware, and explainable results. The system powers two applications: automated structure elucidation (with follow-up experiment planning for tough isomers) and biomarker discovery for cancer, unlocking the biological and clinical potential of the glycome.

Thrust 1: GlycoLLM: Multimodal Foundation Model for Glycan Structure Elucidation

Challenge

Definitive interpretation of tandem MS/MS data remains a critical bottleneck in glycomics, hampered by pervasive isomeric ambiguity, sparse spectral library coverage, and reliance on manual expert-driven analysis that is inherently slow and inconsistent.

Research Approach

  • GlycoLLM: A domain-specialized large language model engineered for joint encoding of spectral data, WURCS/GlycoCT structural graphs, and experimental metadata.
  • Contrastive fragment-structure alignment: Explicitly maps spectral peaks to specific glycan substructures and motifs through multimodal learning.
  • In-silico fragmentation with reranking: Generates candidate structures validated through spectral similarity scoring and structural plausibility assessment.

Expected Outcomes

Superior top-k identification accuracy for novel and isomeric glycans; confidence-aware predictions with selective abstention capabilities; and explainable, step-by-step rationales mapping predicted structural elements to observed spectral evidence.

Thrust 2: GlycoGoT + GlycoKG — Knowledge-Guided Biomarker Discovery with Causal Reasoning

Challenge

Current glycan biomarker discovery often identifies statistically significant signals that fail to replicate across cohorts due to limited mechanistic grounding and insufficient integration of biosynthetic constraints. This results in biomarkers that lack biological plausibility and clinical translatability.

Research Approach

  • GlycoKG Knowledge Graph: Comprehensive encoding of biosynthetic pathways, enzyme specificities, tissue-specific expression patterns, and established disease associations using RDF/SHACL constraints.
  • GlycoGoT Reasoning Engine: A graph-of-thoughts controller that orchestrates multi-step biomarker validation through propose → validate → refine cycles, dynamically routing between GlycoLLM's predictive capabilities and GlycoKG's constraint checking.
  • Multi-Evidence Integration: Bayesian fusion of spectral evidence, structural motifs, glycosylation sites, and clinical annotations with calibrated uncertainty quantification.

Expected Outcomes

Mechanistically grounded biomarkers with explicit pathway-level explanations, demonstrated cross-site generalization in validation cohorts, and clinically actionable insights supported by biosynthetic plausibility.

Unifying Vision

To close the Glycomics Gap by building foundational AI models that automatically read, translate, and reason about the structure and function of glycans, transforming them from analytical challenges into actionable biological insights. Our approach bridges the critical disconnect between mass spectrometry data and biological meaning by teaching AI the 'language' of glycosylation. We are creating systems that not only predict structures from spectra but also explain their biosynthetic pathways and disease relevance. The result is a new paradigm for glycoscience, where AI partners with researchers to accelerate the discovery of glycan-based biomarkers and therapeutic targets.
