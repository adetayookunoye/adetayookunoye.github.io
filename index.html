Research Overview

Glycomics lags behind genomics/proteomics because MS/MS analysis suffers from isomeric ambiguity, sparse libraries, lab variability, and manual, subjective interpretation. We propose an integrated multimodal AI GlycoLLM (encodes spectra + glycan graphs + metadata), GlycoKG (biosynthetic constraints), and GlycoGoT (structured reasoning), that jointly reasons across modalities to produce accurate, confidence-aware, and explainable results. The system powers two applications: automated structure elucidation (with follow-up experiment planning for tough isomers) and biomarker discovery for cancer, unlocking the biological and clinical potential of the glycome.

Thrust 1: GlycoLLM — Multimodal Structure Elucidation

Challenge

Tandem MS/MS data carries isomeric ambiguity and limited library coverage, making expert-driven interpretation slow and inconsistent.

My Approach

  • GlycoLLM: an LLM adapted to jointly encode spectra, WURCS/GlycoCT graphs, and metadata.
  • Contrastive fragment–structure alignment: align peaks to substructures/motifs.
  • In-silico fragmentation + reranking: generate candidates, score with spectral similarity and language likelihood.

Outcomes

Higher top-k accuracy for unknowns, selective abstention with confidence, and step-by-step explanations that map predicted fragments to observed peaks.

Thrust 2: GlycoGoT + GlycoKG — Reasoned, Constraint-Aware Biomarker Discovery

Challenge

Many “significant” glycan signals do not replicate across cohorts or lack mechanistic grounding.

My Approach

  • GlycoKG: biosynthetic pathways, enzyme specificities, tissue expression, and disease links encoded in RDF/SHACL.
  • GlycoGoT: a graph-of-thoughts controller that decomposes analysis into propose → validate → refine, calling GlycoLLM or KG checks as needed.
  • Multi-evidence integration: spectra, structure motifs, glycosites, and clinical labels fused with calibrated uncertainty.

Outcomes

Mechanistically plausible biomarkers with pathway-level explanations and cross-site generalization.

Unifying Vision

I develop AI that not only predicts but also reasons within biochemical rules. By fusing language models with knowledge graphs and tool-use policies, my goal is robust, interpretable glycoscience—from structure calls to clinically actionable signatures.

Research Directions

Direction 1: Structure Elucidation with GlycoLLM

Domain-specialized LLMs for spectra→structure with selective prediction and transparent rationales.

Phase 1: Data & Tokenization

  • Public datasets only (e.g., GlycoPOST, GlyTouCan, GlyConnect, GlyGen context).
  • Joint tokenization for WURCS/GlycoCT and spectral bins; motif fingerprints.

Phase 2: Multimodal Training

  • Masked motif/linkage objectives; spectra↔structure contrastive alignment.
  • In-silico fragmentation supervision for fragment-level faithfulness.

Phase 3: Inference & Reranking

  • Constrained candidate generation (mass/adduct windows), KG-checked plausibility (SHACL).
  • Learned score: spectral similarity + KG priors + LLM likelihood.

Direction 2: Biomarker Discovery with GlycoGoT + GlycoKG

Neuro-symbolic discovery of glycan/glycoprotein signatures for detection, staging, and prognosis.

Aim 1: KG-Grounded Signals

  • Encode enzyme reachability, tissue priors, and pathway context as constraints.
  • Filter spurious signals; surface mechanism-level explanations.

Aim 2: Robust Evaluation

  • Top-k accuracy, risk-coverage with abstention, explanation faithfulness, KG consistency.
  • Biomarker AUC/C-index, calibration, and cross-site transfer.

Aim 3: Reproducible Tools

  • Named-graph provenance, SPARQL APIs, and ready-to-run notebooks for labs.
  • Configurable pipelines for spectra ingestion, structure calls, and cohort analysis.

Broader Impact & Vision

By enforcing biochemical constraints and delivering explainable outputs, my systems aim to become everyday instruments for glyco labs—accelerating structure calls, improving reproducibility, and translating glycan biology into clinical value.
